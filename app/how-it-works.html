How it works

The chain stores the commitment. The browser keeps the person.

Identifiers are normalized and hashed before the live account is written. Discovery then compares local contact hashes against the on-chain registry, which means the raw email or phone number never needs to come back from Solana.

Operating logic

  1. Register an identifier commitment on the deployed program.
  2. Submit a discovery request tied to your wallet.
  3. Load overlap by comparing your local contact hashes with live global commitments.

Privacy boundary

  1. The commitment account is public and verifiable.
  2. The raw email, phone number, and address book stay local.
  3. The result page reveals overlap only, which is the piece the user actually needs.
